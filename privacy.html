Privacy policy

Aphelion. Last updated 21 October 2026. Developer: Robin Nelen.

Aphelion is a single-player gravity game. It does not ask for an account.

What stays on the device

Progress is saved on the device. The game writes one local storage record, key aphelion-career-v1. The record holds a version, the point total, the ids of finished lessons, and whether assist is on. It is not uploaded.

Reset puts the current flight back to its start. It does not delete that record. Deleting the app removes it.

The App Store app does not show the Safari “add to Home Screen” card, and it does not write the session flag that card uses.

What the game does not do

The app loads its screen from a file inside the app. The game code makes no network calls. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no sign-in. Nothing in that save leaves the device.

Haptics are a message from the page to the app on the same phone. They are not sent anywhere else.

Tracking

Aphelion does not track you across apps or websites. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt is not used.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchase and no purchase screen. If the App Store charges for the download, Apple processes that payment. The developer does not receive card details.
