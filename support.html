Aphelion support

A single-player gravity game. Fourteen lessons, then a sandbox.

How do I reset progress?

Reset, on the flight bar, starts the current lesson again. Finished lessons and points stay. Those live in one record on the device, aphelion-career-v1. Deleting the app removes that record. There is no account to delete.

What does each chapter teach?

Fly has three lessons. Watch the rocket, then fly it.

  • First orbit. Park a low orbit and leave fuel in the ship.
  • Deploy. The same parking orbit, then release the satellite.
  • Belly flop. Come home sideways, flip, and meet the tower.

Classroom has seven lessons. See the orbit, then pick it.

  • Tilt to the station. A low orbit tipped to 51.6°.
  • Polar. Over the north pole, then the south.
  • Dawn-dusk. A sun-synchronous pass, the same light each lap.
  • Hangs still. One lap matches a turn of the Earth, so the craft holds one spot.
  • The long ellipse. A low burn raises the far end, then a burn at the top rounds it.
  • Navigation belt. The middle ring, about 14 hours a lap.
  • Linger up high. A long ellipse that stays stretched, slow at the top.

Voyage has four lessons. Watch both burns, then do them.

  • To the Moon. Raise the far end to the Moon, then match its path.
  • Burn home. Slow down at the Moon, then at Earth, and stay on an oval.
  • To Mars. The same two burns, with the Sun in the middle.
  • Home from Mars. Slow down at Mars, then at Earth, and share Earth’s year.
